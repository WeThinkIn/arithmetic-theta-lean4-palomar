# Proof summary

## Proof

The two review main theorems apply the original conservative and cyclotomic
construction results, then project their witnesses through `matrix_family_review`.
The projection retains the same compact curve, structure morphism, half line,
disk, frame, sections, square and normalized generator. It transfers the exact
original SectionImage statement at every cutoff, with one family outside the
universal cutoff quantifier. The finite-level interface follows from the
original all-test representability and exact rank formula. Canonical
normalization and its linkage to the actual metaplectic object are retained
through pointed Cartesian maps and identity-ideal/cotangent equations; the
source comparison and normalization preservation bridges are proved by rfl.

Nine arithmetic equality bridges unify the duplicated parameter APIs without
identifying the conservative and sharp bounds. Generic additive-map kernel
equivalence transfers equality and unique coordinates; its specialization to
actual structural scalar actions transfers all six original section-image
clauses. No new axiom, unproved modularity assumption or abstract expansion
functional is introduced.

## Dependencies

Lean 4.33.1 and the original pinned external dependencies are unchanged. The
original 1,199 source/object pairs and all 103 exact endpoint types and axiom
closures are revalidated. The additive ReviewRoot imports the original GoalRoot
and all 13 new modules. The four development contracts and the combined contract
record 18 exported exact types. The final Wujie audit and portable reproduction
record current source/object/compiler bindings separately from historical evidence.

## Limitations

New main statements are proved projections of richer original construction
witnesses, not equivalent replacements for the entire MatrixData record.
Explicit torsor/fiber construction data, conditional surjectivity, exact fiber
rank identities and auxiliary unary/coset construction witnesses remain in the
unchanged originals. The new finite-level interface retains representability,
finite etaleness, actual maps/naturality and the rank bound. No reverse
reconstruction of every original construction witness is claimed. Mechanical
checking and reduced statement dependencies do not establish independent human
semantic acceptance or compress the original proof closure.

## Integration

ReviewRoot jointly imports the original proof and every new module. Its distinct
name preserves the original GoalRoot verbatim. The Wujie audit uses the leaf
delivery policy for this additive library, compiles its actual ReviewRoot and
checks every frozen export. It is accompanied by the fresh original 103-endpoint
recheck; no original proof module needs recompilation when its prior source/object
binding remains valid.

## Coverage changes

The original 103 endpoints and 13 source requirements are unchanged. Added scope:
nine arithmetic bridges, three relation/image transfer results, one finite-level
projection, two canonical-normalization preservation results, one source-family
projection, and two review main theorems. These are additive mathematical APIs,
not a new claim that the Palomar or human semantic review is complete.

## Remaining gaps

All exported obligations of this additive review layer have source proofs and
are covered by its exact-type and axiom checks. Independent human review of the
geometric mathematics remains an external review activity. Further compression
of the complete original construction record, or a comparison with analytic
Mathlib ModularForm objects, is outside this change and is not silently assumed.
