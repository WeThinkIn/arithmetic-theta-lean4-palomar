- Gram validity includes symmetry, integral even diagonal and positive definiteness of xᵀAx/2.
- RationalCosets is the concrete B⁻¹Zⁿ/Zⁿ additive quotient. Representatives is the actual quotient-class bijection, not an arbitrary finite set.
- OrthogonalData asserts det B≠0, positive even m_j, and BᵀAB=diag(m); it is the source sublattice hypothesis in 025–026. Endpoint 027 obtains the corresponding data for the actual deterministic algorithm from its provider without assuming them as an extra premise.

- All original quantifiers over D, its nonzeroness proof, and divisibility `2*m_j ∣ D` remain. The expansion exponent is exactly `D/(2*m_j)`. There is no chosen special denominator substituted for the universal statement.
- Endpoint 026 retains the unscaled theta identity as well as the expanded identity.
- Endpoint 027 retains all c in C^h, including dependent/deficient-rank families. No linear independence premise is introduced.
- Endpoint 024 retains the original universe-polymorphic field, characteristic zero, and residue congruence hypotheses.
